# RagKno

> RagKno is an open-source retrieval-augmented generation workspace for asking questions about documents, public web pages, and Google Drive files, with streamed answers and source citations.

## Product

- Upload PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents, add public web pages, or import supported Google Drive content.
- Select sources for a question, retrieve relevant passages, and inspect citations alongside the answer.
- Manage conversations and indexed sources in an authenticated, user-scoped workspace.
- Answers may be incomplete or inaccurate; verify important claims against the cited sources.
